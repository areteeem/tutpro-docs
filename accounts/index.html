TutPro accounts

One sign-in for teaching and studying

TutPro credentials, email confirmation, Google sign-in and password recovery all live at www.tutpro.org/signin.

Choose the right suite

If you follow a product link, TutPro returns you to that product after sign-in. Without a destination, people with both Teacher and Student access choose a suite. Teacher-only accounts open Teacher; Student-only accounts open Student. Creating a Teacher account alone does not grant Student access—you need a provider invitation.

Recover or switch accounts

Use Forgot password on the TutPro sign-in page, or sign in with the same Google account you used originally. When accepting a student invitation, the join page keeps your place through recovery and verification. If the invitation was sent to another email, select Switch TutPro account on the join page.

Teachers inviting students

Create or locate the student in the correct school or private-teacher workspace. Open that student’s card and copy the student invitation. A new link revokes the previous unused link, expires in seven days and can be accepted once. Ask the student to complete it themselves; never create an account from roster email alone.
