# Accounts and sign-in

TutPro account creation, Google sign-in, email confirmation, and password recovery start at [TutPro sign-in](https://www.tutpro.org/signin).

## Choose the right app

If you open a product link before signing in, TutPro returns you to that product afterward. If your account has both Teacher and Student access and you open the account home, choose the workspace you want. A Teacher account does not automatically create Student access; a school or teacher must invite you to a Student workspace.

## Recover or switch accounts

1. Open [TutPro sign-in](https://www.tutpro.org/signin) and choose **Forgot password** if you cannot sign in.
2. Use the same email or Google account you used originally.
3. For an invitation sent to another account, open the invitation and choose **Switch TutPro account**. The invitation remains available while you sign in to the correct account.

## Invite a student

1. In Teacher, open **Students** and select the student in the correct personal or school workspace.
2. Open the student profile and choose the invitation action.
3. Send the student the new link privately. A link is single-use, expires after seven days, and issuing another link replaces the previous unused one.
4. Ask the student to complete sign-in and accept the invitation themselves. Do not create an account from a roster email alone.
