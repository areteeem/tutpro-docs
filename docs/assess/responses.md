# Responses and results

Choose **View responses** from the builder to review submissions. Select a response and inspect its answers, automatic points, and any teacher-only review details.

## Review scoring

Check that the respondent and attempt match the intended assignment. Review automatic scoring for text or ambiguous answers before publishing. Correct scores or add teacher feedback using the response controls.

## Publish results

Use the publish/release control provided by the response view after review. Students may not see scores or explanations until you release them, depending on quiz settings.

## Check scanned work

For paper submissions, open **Check PDFs** to use AutoCheck. Follow the page’s upload and matching steps, inspect any extracted answer or score, and confirm uncertain results manually. See the separate [AutoCheck tutorial](./autocheck).
