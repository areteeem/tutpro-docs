# Library and decks

The Decks home page is your library. Choose a deck to open its cards, search or organize your collection, or use a teacher-assigned deck from its Student assignment.

Decks you own and decks shared with you can have different edit controls. Open a deck to check its description, card count, and available study actions. Use a descriptive title so the deck is easy to find when assigning it from Teacher.
