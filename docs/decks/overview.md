# Overview

Decks is TutPro’s flashcard practice app. Teachers organize cards into decks and can assign practice; students review assigned cards and practice recall over time.

<div className="guide-feature-note"><p><strong>Decks features can change.</strong> Study modes and assignment options may vary by release and account role. We’ll update this guide as soon as possible when the available features change.</p></div>

## A typical flow

1. Open [Decks](https://decks.tutpro.org) through your TutPro account.
2. Create a deck for a topic or course and add cards with the prompt on one side and the answer on the other.
3. Review the deck using the available study flow.
4. If you teach, open the teacher view to assign the deck to a student or group.
5. Students open the assignment and review cards. Their recall choices guide the next review schedule.

## Decks in TutPro assignments

From the Teacher workspace, open a student or lesson and use **Add deck**. Select the deck and confirm the assignment target. Decks assignments remain distinct from a plain link or uploaded document. In Student, open the assignment from the student work area.

## Sign-in and account linking

Use your TutPro account entry point when you open Decks from the suite. A student must have a Student workspace invitation and an appropriate deck assignment. Ask the teacher to confirm the assignment if the deck is not shown.
