# Progress and review queue

Open the progress view or a deck’s due queue to see what needs practice. Review new, due, mastered, and overdue cards using the labels the app displays.

Your recall rating helps calculate the next review interval when spaced repetition is active. If your assigned deck does not show the expected progress, confirm you signed into the same TutPro account and opened the assigned workspace.

Teachers can inspect a student’s card browser or assignment progress from the student or group page when their role permits it.
