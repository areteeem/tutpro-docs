# Progress and review queue

## Review your own card progress

1. Open **Progress** in Decks, or open a deck and choose its due/review queue.
2. Check the new, due, mastered, and overdue counts shown by the app.
3. Open the due cards and try to recall each answer before revealing it.
4. Rate your recall honestly. When spaced repetition is active, that rating helps set the next review interval.
5. Return to Progress to see updated counts. If an assigned deck is missing, confirm that you opened it from Student and are signed in to the same account.

Your recall rating helps calculate the next review interval when spaced repetition is active. If your assigned deck does not show the expected progress, confirm you signed into the same TutPro account and opened the assigned workspace.

## Review a learner’s progress as a teacher

1. Open the learner or group record in Teacher, or open the Decks assignment in Tasks.
2. Choose the Decks progress or card-browser action available for that record.
3. Check completion status and the cards that need review. For a group, compare learner status before following up.
4. If the expected activity is not visible, verify the student account, assigned deck, and provider workspace.

Teachers can inspect a student’s card browser or assignment progress from the student or group page when their role permits it.
