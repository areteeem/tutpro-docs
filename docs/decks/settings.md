# Settings and account

Open **Settings** to review Decks preferences such as language, appearance, study behavior, and notifications when available. Sign-in and account identity are shared through the TutPro account entry point.

If assigned decks are missing, check which TutPro account is active and whether the provider workspace is connected. Do not create a second account to work around an invitation issue.
