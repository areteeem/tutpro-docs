# Student assignments

1. Sign in to **Student** with the account that received the assignment.
2. Open **Homework** and select the deck assignment. Check its instructions and due date.
3. Choose the deck link or **Start** action. It opens the linked deck in Decks under the same account.
4. Read the front of a card and try to recall the answer before revealing it.
5. Reveal the back, compare it with your answer, and choose the recall rating that best reflects how well you remembered it.
6. Continue through the assigned cards. Use the due queue to review cards ready for practice, and return to unfinished work from the assignment.
7. Go back to Student to check the assignment status after your session.

If the deck does not open, confirm that you accepted the provider invitation and are signed in to that account. Ask the teacher to check the assignment target and due date.
