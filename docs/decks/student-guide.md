# Student assignments

Open the deck assignment from **Student → Homework**. It opens the linked deck in Decks under the same TutPro identity.

Read the prompt, try to recall the answer, reveal it, and choose the recall response that best matches your effort. Use the due queue to review cards that are ready for practice. Return later to finish unfinished work.

If the deck does not open, confirm that you accepted the provider invitation and are signed in to that account. Ask the teacher to check the assignment target and due date.
