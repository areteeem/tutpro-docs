# Study modes

Open a deck to choose among its available study actions. The app may offer **Learn new**, **Practice due**, **Quiz**, and **Study** modes. Student assignments can also provide mixed or cross-deck study.

## Learn new cards

Read the prompt, recall the answer, reveal the back, and follow the on-screen control to mark the card. New-card controls can include wheel/spin or other study interaction depending on the deck settings.


![Learn mode card with a term and reveal action.](/img/screenshots/decks-learn-card.png)
![Spin Wheel study activity.](/img/screenshots/decks-spin-wheel.png)

## Practice due cards

Work through the review queue and rate how well you recalled each card. Your response updates the next review timing when spaced repetition is enabled. Retry difficult cards until you can recall them without reading the back first.


![Study session options for card pool, exercise mode, and answer order.](/img/screenshots/decks-study-session.png)

## Quiz and mixed study

Choose **Quiz** to test yourself with the quiz flow. Assigned students may also use mixed practice across the assigned deck. Finish the session and review missed terms before closing.
