# Study modes

Open a deck and choose among its available study actions. The app may offer **Learn new**, **Practice due**, **Quiz**, and **Study** modes. Student assignments can also provide mixed or cross-deck study. The exact controls depend on the deck and activity selected.

## Learn new cards

1. Choose **Learn new** to start with cards you have not studied.
2. Read the prompt and try to remember the answer before revealing the back.
3. Reveal the answer and compare it with what you recalled.
4. Use the displayed rating or activity control to continue. Some decks offer a wheel/spin interaction or other study activity.
5. Repeat with each new card, then use **Practice due** later to revisit scheduled cards.

## Practice due cards

1. Choose **Practice due** or open the due queue for the deck.
2. Read the front of each card and attempt recall before revealing the answer.
3. Reveal the back and select the rating that best matches your recall.
4. Continue through the queue. Your response updates the next review timing when spaced repetition is enabled.
5. Retry difficult cards until you can recall them without reading the back first.


![Study session options for card pool, exercise mode, and answer order.](/img/screenshots/decks-study-session.png)

## Quiz and mixed study

1. Choose **Quiz** to test recall using the quiz flow, or select **Study** for the activity options available to that deck.
2. If prompted, choose the card pool, exercise type, and answer order.
3. Answer each prompt before checking the result. In matching activities, pair the related terms; in true/false or multiple choice, select your response and review the feedback.
4. Finish the session and review missed terms before closing.
5. Assigned students may also use mixed practice across the assigned deck; open that activity from the assignment so progress stays associated with the assigned work.
