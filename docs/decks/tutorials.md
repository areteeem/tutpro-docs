# Decks tutorials

Use these walkthroughs for common Decks workflows. Product names and buttons can vary slightly by workspace role.

## Create a deck and add cards

1. Open [Decks](https://decks.tutpro.org) and choose **Create deck** or the add button in your library.
2. Enter a deck title and any subject or collection details the editor requests.
3. Add a card. Put a prompt, question, or term on the front and the answer or explanation on the back.
4. Add the next card, or use quick-add or import to enter several cards. Check the front/back mapping in the preview before confirming an import.
5. Review the deck for duplicate cards, unclear prompts, and missing answers, then save.
6. Open the deck and try a few cards before sharing or assigning it.

See [Build and edit a deck](./build-and-study) for card formatting and bulk import.

## Share a deck with a link

1. Open the deck you want to share and select **Share**.
2. Choose the available access level: viewing, copying, or editing. Use the least access the recipient needs.
3. Create or copy the share link shown by Decks.
4. Open the link in a private or signed-out browser window to confirm what a recipient can see and which actions are available.
5. Send the link through your usual channel. Tell recipients to use **Copy** if they need their own editable version.

A shared link gives access according to its link settings. A Teacher assignment is different: it targets selected students and keeps the deck attached to their assigned work. See [Shared decks](./shared-decks).

## Assign a deck to students from Teacher

1. In [Teacher](https://www.tutpro.org), open **Tasks** or open the intended student, group, or lesson.
2. Choose **Assign task** if you are in Tasks, then fill in the title and student-facing instructions.
3. In attachments or task items, choose **Add deck**. Do not paste the deck URL as a normal link when you want tracked Decks work.
4. Select the deck and confirm the student or group receiving it.
5. Set the assigned date and due date, plus any study requirements shown in the form.
6. Preview the task, save it, and reopen it to verify the deck, recipient, and due date.
7. Ask the student to open it from **Student → Homework**. That route keeps the assignment linked to the learner’s progress.

See [Teacher assignments](./teacher-guide) and [Create homework](../teacher/homework-create) for the full assignment flow.

## Complete an assigned deck as a student

1. Sign in to [Student](https://student.tutpro.org) and select the workspace where the teacher assigned the work.
2. Open **Homework** or **Tasks**, then select the Decks assignment.
3. Choose **Open deck** from the assignment. Check the new-card or due-card count.
4. Select a study activity. Try to recall each answer before revealing it.
5. For recall-based review, choose the response that best matches how well you remembered the card.
6. Continue until the session finishes, then return to Student and check the assignment status.

See [Student assignments](./student-guide) and [Study modes](./study-modes).

## Choose a study mode

1. Open a deck and check whether you want to learn new cards, review cards that are due, or test recall.
2. Choose **Learn new** for cards you have not practiced, **Practice due** for scheduled reviews, or **Quiz** to test yourself. The exact options depend on the deck and assignment.
3. Read the prompt and try to answer before revealing the back or selecting an answer.
4. Follow the activity instructions. When asked to rate recall, choose honestly so the next review interval is useful.
5. Finish the session and review difficult cards again.

More detail and activity examples are in [Study modes](./study-modes).

## Check progress and review cards

1. Open **Progress** in Decks or open the assigned deck’s due queue.
2. Review new, due, mastered, or overdue counts to choose what to practice next.
3. Select a card or queue to see its current status and complete the available review.
4. Rate your recall honestly; when spaced repetition is active, that rating helps schedule the next review.
5. Teachers can open the learner’s card browser or assignment from Teacher to review completion and card-level progress.

If the assignment or progress is missing, confirm the Student workspace and the assignment target before creating a duplicate. See [Progress and review queue](./progress).
