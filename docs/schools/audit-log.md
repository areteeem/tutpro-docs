# Audit log

The school audit log helps authorized administrators review important changes and investigate unexpected activity. Open **Administration → Audit Log** in the Teacher workspace.

## Review activity

1. Choose a time range and narrow the list by actor, action, or record when those filters are available.
2. Open an event to review its timestamp, actor, action, and affected record.
3. Compare nearby events to understand the sequence; an audit entry is evidence of an event, not an undo control.
4. Use [Data tools](./data-tools) for exports and [Security](./security) for access settings.

Use the log when checking staff changes, student access, schedule edits, or administrative actions. Visibility depends on your school role.
