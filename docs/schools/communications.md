# School communications and calendar

Open **Administration → Communications** for school announcements, academic events, departments, and related analytics where enabled. Use **Feed** for posts managed within a teacher’s audience.

## Publish an announcement

Choose **Add announcement**, enter the message and priority, then choose its audience and any teacher/campus targeting. Preview the message and confirm the dates before publishing. Broad school announcements should not include private student records.


## Create and assign a school task

Use the school task action to assign follow-up work to a staff member. Add a clear title and due date, select the responsible person, and review the assignment before saving. Keep task details limited to what the assigned staff member needs to act.

![School task form with an assignee and due date.](/img/screenshots/schools-create-task.png)

## Add an academic event

Enter the event name, start and end dates, event type, and color. Check the school calendar after saving and confirm the intended audience understands whether lessons are affected.

## Manage departments

Create or edit a department and select its lead where available. Confirm the staff member is linked to the correct department after saving.

## Review activity

Use announcement/calendar analytics to inspect delivery and engagement. Open the audit log when you need the record of who changed the configuration.
