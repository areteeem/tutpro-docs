# Create a school workspace

Open **Administration → Schools & Campuses** and choose **Create school workspace** when your account is allowed to create one.

1. Follow the three setup steps and enter the school name and required organization details.
2. Add an initial campus if the wizard offers it, then verify its name and time zone.
3. Add the first staff member only after selecting the intended role.
4. Review the summary and finish the wizard.
5. Confirm the new school appears in the workspace switcher and open its Administration page.

The initial wizard establishes the organization; finish room setup, staff invitations, pricing, and grading preferences in their dedicated sections. If the wizard reports an error, keep the message and check whether the school already appears before trying again to avoid duplicates.
