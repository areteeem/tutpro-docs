# School administration overview

School features are available in the Teacher app’s **Administration** area according to your school role and capabilities. The separate Schools console is a narrower, read-only schedule and audit dashboard for an assigned school.

## Choose the right surface

- Use **Teacher → Administration** to manage school settings, staff, roles, students, campuses, resources, finances, reporting, and school communications.
- Use the **Schools console** to view the assigned school schedule and, when permitted, its audit log. It has refresh and pagination controls and does not provide the full administration tools.
- Use the regular Teacher pages for teaching work such as a lesson, homework task, grading, or a student profile.


![Schools and campuses administration view.](/img/screenshots/schools-overview.png)

## Recommended setup order

1. Create or open the school workspace.
2. Add campuses, rooms, and equipment.
3. Invite staff with the least access needed for their role.
4. Add students and assign teacher/campus ownership.
5. Set schedule and pricing defaults.
6. Review finance, communications, certificates, reports, and audit settings.

An owner’s access is school-wide. Other roles are limited by their permissions and campus scope. A menu item being visible does not guarantee that every record can be edited; the server checks access when an action is saved.
