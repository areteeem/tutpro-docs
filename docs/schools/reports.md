# School reports and KPIs

Open **Administration → Reports** for school-level metrics available to your role. Choose the date range, campus, and teacher scope before comparing results.

## Review a metric

Open a KPI to see the relevant source records when that action is available. Check lesson statuses, attendance, assignment history, and recognized payment dates before interpreting changes.


![School expenses report with date range and export.](/img/screenshots/schools-expenses-report.png)

## Compare staff or campuses

Use teacher and campus filters to examine workload or lesson delivery. Confirm the sample size and time range are equivalent before making comparisons.

## Export a report

Preview the selected scope and totals, then export or print. Keep downloads private and reconcile finance figures with the payment and expense ledgers.
