# School schedule and room planning

Open **Administration → Schedule** to inspect school lessons by date and resource. Choose a teacher or room view, select a campus, and move through the day or week.

## Find schedule conflicts

Review lessons and resource use for the chosen date. Check teacher availability, campus time zones, room occupancy, and group size. Use filters to isolate a campus or resource when the view is busy.


![School schedule with room and resource utilization.](/img/screenshots/schools-schedule.png)
![School schedule filters and free-time actions.](/img/screenshots/schools-schedule-filters.png)
![Filter the shared school schedule.](/img/screenshots/schools-schedule-filter-dialog.png)

## Find an available room

Open the vacancy finder, enter the time range and group size, and choose equipment requirements. TutPro lists rooms available for the full interval. Open a room’s week view to inspect nearby bookings.


![Available classrooms and equipment for a time range.](/img/screenshots/schools-room-availability.png)

## Book a lesson

Choose **Book lesson** on a free room result. The schedule editor opens with the selected time and room. Complete the student/group and teaching details, then save and confirm the event appears in the resource schedule.

## Move an existing lesson

Open the lesson and edit its date, time, teacher, room, or campus. Review conflicts again after saving. If a resource is under maintenance, block the affected period so it is not suggested as available.
