# Security

School security controls are available to authorized administrators from **Administration → Security**. Controls shown depend on the school configuration and your role.

## Review access

1. Confirm staff membership and role assignments in [Roles and access](./roles-and-access).
2. Remove or correct access that is no longer needed.
3. Review authentication and account safeguards available to your school.
4. Check the [Audit log](./audit-log) after a sensitive change.

If a person can see data they should not, remove the relevant access promptly and contact the school owner or support. Do not share passwords, invite links, or exported student data in a public channel.
