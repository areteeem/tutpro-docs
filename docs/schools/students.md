# School students and assignments

Open **Administration → Students** to review school student ownership and assignments. Use the regular **Students** page for everyday profile and teaching work.


![School student directory and assignment actions.](/img/screenshots/schools-students.png)

## Add or assign a student

Create the student record or select an existing student. Choose the responsible teacher and current campus, then review the student’s profile before saving. Student accounts can connect to more than one provider workspace, so confirm the correct school membership.

## Change teacher or campus

Open the student’s assignment controls and select the new owner or campus. Review future lessons at the former campus before confirming a move; reschedule affected lessons first. The current campus is the active assignment, and campus history records changes where enabled.

## Invite the learner

Issue a Student invitation from the learner’s profile and share it privately. The student accepts with their own TutPro account. Do not send invitation links in public school communications.

## Review history

Open the student record to inspect ownership, campus history, and connected activity available to your role. Use reports or the activity history for broader review.
