# Achievements

Open **Achievements** to see milestones available to your account. Achievement rules and visibility are set by the app and provider. Select an item for its description when available.

Achievements are recognition markers; they do not replace grades, certificates, or a teacher’s assessment. If the page is empty, there may be no milestones to show yet.
