# Overview

Student is the learner-facing TutPro app. It shows a safe projection of the information your teacher or school shares with your account; it is not the full teacher workspace.

## What you can do

- Review upcoming lessons and lesson history.
- Open homework, submit work, and read feedback or grades.
- Check payment and invoice status shared by your provider.
- Read teacher posts and updates.
- Edit selected profile information and send requests where enabled.
- Switch between provider workspaces you have joined.
- Open assigned Decks or assessments from their assignment links.

<div className="guide-feature-note"><p><strong>Student features can change.</strong> Available tabs depend on your provider’s setup and your account access. We’ll update this guide as soon as possible when the app changes; use the Student app’s current navigation as the source of truth.</p></div>

## Sign in for the first time

1. Open [Student](https://student.tutpro.org).
2. Sign in with the TutPro account that accepted the provider invitation.
3. Choose the provider workspace if you have more than one.
4. Review the Home or Schedule view for upcoming lessons and the Tasks area for assigned work.

If you do not have a workspace yet, request an invitation from the school or teacher. A standalone Teacher account does not create a student connection.

## Offline behavior

Student can use locally cached data to open in a limited read-only state when the network is unavailable. Student-generated updates and requests can queue until the connection returns. Confirm the sync state before assuming a profile update has reached your provider.

## Privacy

Your Student app shows information for the active provider and account. Leaving or losing access to one provider does not disable your TutPro account or other provider workspaces.
