# Certificates

Open **Certificates** to view certificates issued to your Student workspace. Select a certificate to review its details or open the document if a download/view action is available.

If no certificate is listed, your provider may not have issued one yet or may not have enabled the section. Contact your teacher if you believe a certificate is missing.
