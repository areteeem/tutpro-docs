# Home

Open **Home** after signing in to see a short summary of your Student workspace. Cards can include upcoming lessons, homework due soon, payment information shared by your provider, and quick links to other sections.

Choose a card to open its full lesson or task details. If a card is missing, check the active provider workspace and ask your teacher whether that section is enabled.
