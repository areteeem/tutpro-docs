# Decks

The Student app links to assigned flashcard work in Decks. Open **Decks** from the student navigation or assignment link.

## Study assigned cards

1. Open the assigned deck and check its due or new-card count.
2. Choose the available study activity.
3. Read the prompt, reveal or enter your answer, then record how well you knew it.
4. Continue until the session ends; progress is saved to your account when online.

For review timing and activity choices, see [Study modes](../decks/study-modes). For the main Student app, see [Overview](./app).
