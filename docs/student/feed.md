# Feed

Open **Feed** to read posts shared with your provider workspace. Unread indicators mark updates you have not opened. Select a post for its full text and use a response action when the author has enabled one.

The feed is scoped to the active provider. Switch workspaces to check another teacher or school’s updates. Avoid sharing a post outside its intended audience if it contains class-specific information.
