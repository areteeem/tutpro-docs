# Grades and feedback

Open **Progress → Grades** to review grades shared by your provider. Choose a result to see its title, score, date, and teacher feedback when available.

The grade may be a percentage, points out of a maximum, or another scale selected by your teacher. Compare it with the displayed maximum rather than assuming every class uses the same scale.

A missing grade can mean the item has not been reviewed, the provider has not released the result, or the assignment is not gradeable. Contact your teacher if the status remains unclear.
