# Join a Student workspace

Ask your teacher or school for a personal invitation. TutPro sign-in and account creation happen at [TutPro](https://www.tutpro.org).

1. Open the invitation from your teacher or school. A new invitation is valid for seven days and replaces any earlier unused invitation for that student record.
2. Sign in to your existing TutPro account, or create one and confirm your email. If you also teach, use the same account.
3. Review the provider and any profile fields you choose to copy from another workspace.
4. Confirm **Join**. TutPro opens Student with this provider available in the workspace switcher.

## If the link does not work

- **Wrong account:** choose **Switch TutPro account** on the join page. The invitation stays available.
- **Expired or replaced:** ask the provider to issue a fresh link and send only the latest one.
- **Already used:** sign in to the account that accepted it. If the wrong account accepted, contact the provider.
- **Old sid/sn link:** these legacy links do not authenticate. Ask for a new TutPro invitation.
- **Temporary problem:** retry later and send your provider the error text, not the secret invitation URL.

The receiving provider gets only the profile fields you approve. Lessons, homework, grades, payments, and internal notes are not transferred.
