# Payments

Open **Payments** to review payment details your teacher or school has chosen to share. Select a payment or invoice to see its available status, date, amount, and description.

The provider controls whether amounts are visible. Viewing an invoice does not send payment or update its status. Use the payment method or instructions supplied by your provider, then contact them if a receipt or balance looks incorrect.
