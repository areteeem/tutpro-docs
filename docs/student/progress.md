# Progress

1. Open **Progress** from the Student navigation.
2. Review the overview for recent lessons, homework activity, progress trends, and subject or skill summaries shared by your provider.
3. Use the section control to switch between overview, grades, or skills.
4. Select a chart or result when the app offers a detail view, then check the dates and assignment status behind the summary.
5. If a section is empty or a result looks unexpected, check that you are in the right provider workspace and ask your teacher whether the data has been shared.

Charts summarize the information available to Student; they may not include every classroom record.

If a section is empty, there may be no shared data yet, or the provider may have hidden that section. Ask your teacher if you expected a result to appear.
