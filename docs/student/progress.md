# Progress

Open **Progress** for the sections shared by your provider. The overview can include recent lessons, homework activity, progress trends, and subject/skill summaries.

Use the section control to switch between overview, grades, or skills. Select a chart or result when the app offers details. A chart summarizes the information available to Student; it may not include every classroom record.

If a section is empty, there may be no shared data yet, or the provider may have hidden that section. Ask your teacher if you expected a result to appear.
