# Schedule

Open **Schedule** to review your upcoming lessons and lesson history shared by the active provider. Switch the date range or calendar view to find a lesson. Select a lesson for its time, teacher, topic, and location details when available.

Lesson times may use your provider’s or campus time zone. If the date or time looks unexpected, check the displayed time zone and contact your teacher before assuming a lesson has moved.

A request to change a lesson does not change the calendar automatically. Use the request/contact action if enabled and wait for your provider to confirm the update.
