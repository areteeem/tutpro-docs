# Student settings and notifications

Open **Settings** from the Student navigation or **More**. Use this page to choose language and appearance, configure personal notifications, and manage the current provider workspace or TutPro account. Your teacher or school controls which teaching sections appear in your navigation.

## Change language or appearance

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Choose **Language** and select **Українська** or **English**. Labels update immediately.
3. Choose **Appearance** and select **System**, **Light**, or **Dark**. **System** follows your device’s appearance preference.
4. These preferences are stored in this browser profile. Set them again on another browser or device if needed.

## Choose notification preferences

The **Notifications** section has individual switches:

- **Lesson rating prompts** controls the optional prompt to rate a completed lesson.
- **Homework reminders** controls in-app reminders for homework due today.
- **Lesson reminders (30 min)** controls the 30-minute lesson reminder.
- **Feed post alerts** controls device alerts for new teacher posts.
- **Grade alerts** controls device alerts for new grades and homework feedback.
- **Device notifications** enables browser push for this Student account on this browser.

To enable device notifications:

1. Open **Settings → Notifications**.
2. Read the **Browser permission** status row. **Not requested** means the browser has not asked yet. **Blocked in browser** means permission must be changed in the browser’s site settings. **Unsupported** means this browser cannot receive web push.
3. Turn on **Device notifications** and allow the browser prompt.
4. Confirm the row says **Allowed · push on**. The subscription is specific to this browser and device.
5. Keep browser and operating-system notifications enabled. Server-generated push can arrive while Student is closed when the browser, device, and network support background delivery. Local reminders are created while Student is running.

If you block the permission by mistake, change it in the browser’s site settings for `student.tutpro.org`, return to Student, and enable the switch again. Notification preferences are saved in this browser profile; push subscriptions are registered to your signed-in Student account.

## Understand the Student tabs

Your teacher or school chooses which shared teaching sections are visible. The Student **Dashboard** and **Settings** stay available. **Grades** and **Stats** are sections within **Progress**; the Progress page appears when the provider enables one or both. The navigation may place less-used pages under **More** on a phone.

If a section is missing, ask your teacher to check **Teacher → Settings → Teaching & student app → Visible tabs in student app**. After the teacher changes access, refresh Student to load the updated workspace settings.

## Account and provider workspace

The account area in Settings can show your active provider workspace and actions such as adding Teacher access, leaving the current provider, signing out, or deleting your TutPro account. Leaving one provider removes access to that provider’s shared records; it does not remove other provider workspaces. Account deletion is separate and permanent. Read the confirmation details before continuing.

Profile details are managed from **Profile**. Some fields are controlled by your teacher or school and cannot be edited here.
