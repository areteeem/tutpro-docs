# Settings

Open **Settings** to adjust the preferences available for your account. These may include language, appearance, and notifications. Some settings are controlled by your provider and cannot be changed from Student.

After changing a preference, return to the app and confirm the update. Profile information is managed separately from appearance and notification settings.
