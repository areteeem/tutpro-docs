# Skills

Open **Progress → Skills** to see subject or competency summaries where your provider has entered skill information. The chart helps you notice areas of growth and topics to review.

Use the displayed labels and time range to interpret the result. A skill with no score may not have been assessed yet. Use lesson notes and teacher feedback for next steps.
