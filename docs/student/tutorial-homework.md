# Student tutorial: complete homework

Use this flow when your teacher assigns a task in Student.

## Open the assignment

1. Sign in to [Student](https://student.tutpro.org) and choose the correct provider workspace.
2. Open **Tasks** or **Homework** from the main navigation.
3. Select the task to read its instructions, due date, links, and attachments.

## Complete and submit

Work through the instructions. If the task includes an attached Deck or assessment, open that assignment from its action link and return to Student when you finish. Add your response or file in the homework submission area and choose **Submit**.

Check that the task status changes or a submission confirmation appears. If it does not, check your connection and reopen the task before submitting again.

## Read feedback

Return to the assignment after your teacher reviews it. Open the feedback or grade section to read comments and any score. If you need clarification, use the communication method your teacher has provided.

## If the task is missing

- Confirm the selected provider workspace.
- Clear any status or course filters.
- Check the due date and completed-task section.
- Ask your teacher to confirm that the work was assigned to your Student account.
