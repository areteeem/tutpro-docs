# Student tutorial: switch workspaces

Student keeps each teacher or school in its own workspace so records remain separated by provider.

1. Open [Student](https://student.tutpro.org) and sign in.
2. Select the workspace name or switcher in the app shell.
3. Choose the provider whose schedule or homework you need.
4. Confirm the provider name before opening a task or profile.

To add another provider, request a separate invitation and accept it with your TutPro account. To leave a provider, open your profile, select that provider, and choose **Remove school or teacher**. The action removes access to that workspace; it does not close your TutPro account.

If a workspace is missing, re-open the invitation using the invited account or contact the provider. See [join a workspace](./join) for invitation help.
