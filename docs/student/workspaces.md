# Student workspaces and provider access

Each school or private teacher is a separate Student workspace. The workspace switcher changes which provider’s schedule, homework, feedback, and profile you are viewing.

## Join another provider

Ask the receiving provider for a new invitation. Accepting it adds a workspace; it does not remove your existing workspace. Profile details are copied only after you review and approve them. Later changes in one workspace do not silently change another.

## Switch providers

Open the workspace switcher in Student and choose the provider you want. If the expected provider is missing, confirm that you accepted its invitation using the right TutPro account.

## Leave a provider

From the Student profile, choose the provider and select **Remove school or teacher**. Access to that workspace ends. The provider retains its records under its retention rules, and your other Student workspaces and Teacher access stay connected to your account.

## If a provider disables access

Only that provider workspace becomes inactive. It stops loading in Student and its locally cached portal data is cleared. Contact the provider if you need access restored.

> A school does not own or disable your TutPro account. Never share your password or a one-time invitation link.
