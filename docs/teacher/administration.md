# Administration

Administration contains workspace and school controls. The sections shown depend on your account role and whether your workspace belongs to a school.

## Choose an administration area

- [Settings](./settings) — review workspace preferences.
- [Data tools](./data-tools) — import, export, or manage available data operations.
- [Schools overview](../schools/overview) — manage a school, campuses, staff, and shared operations.
- [Roles and access](../schools/roles-and-access) — review school roles and permissions.
- [Security](../schools/security) — review available security controls.
- [Audit log](../schools/audit-log) — inspect recorded administrative activity.

Only make access changes when you have the required role. Confirm the selected school or campus before applying a change.
