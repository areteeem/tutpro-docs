# Copilot and lesson planning

Copilot uses AI to answer teaching questions and prepare drafts from the workspace context you choose. It can use a lesson or student context when you open it from that workflow, and you can attach relevant records yourself. Review each response; Copilot can be wrong or miss context.

## Ask a question or prepare a draft

1. Open **Copilot** from Teacher, or choose **Ask Copilot** from a lesson, student, or other supported workspace page.
2. Check which lesson, student, or records are included in the current context. Attach only the additional context needed for your request.
3. Ask one focused question or name the draft you need, such as a lesson outline, homework instructions, or practice questions.
4. Review the answer and any linked records. Check facts, student level, curriculum fit, tone, and proposed dates or scores.
5. If Copilot suggests a lesson, assignment, or schedule change, open the relevant editor, revise the proposal, and save it yourself. Copilot responses are suggestions; they do not directly create or change workspace records.

## What AI receives

Copilot sends your prompt and selected or relevant workspace context to the configured AI service through TutPro’s server. Images you attach are sent as part of that request. The response is generated by an external AI service, not entirely on your device. Avoid including passwords, sensitive student details, or confidential records unless your organization has approved that use. Check your organization’s AI and data policies before attaching student context.

Generated teaching material is a draft. Check every answer key and factual claim before sharing it with students.
