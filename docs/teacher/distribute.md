# Distribute payments

Use **Payments → Distribute** when one receipt covers multiple lessons. The tool helps map a received amount to the records it pays.

1. Select the student or source payment and review the amount available.
2. Choose the unpaid or partially paid lesson records to include.
3. Review the amount assigned to each record and any remaining balance.
4. Save the distribution only when the allocations match your receipt.
5. Reopen the payment and lesson details to confirm their statuses and balances.

For a group lesson, check attendance and each student’s share before allocating. If the source payment is not visible, clear filters and check the active workspace, student, date range, and payment status.
