# Expenses

Open **Payments → Expenses** to review costs recorded for the active workspace. School expense access and editing may be limited by role and campus scope.

## Add an expense

Enter the date, category, amount, and a clear note. In school mode, select the relevant campus when the form provides that field. Save and verify the entry appears in the list and monthly total.

## Edit or remove an expense

Open the expense record and confirm the date, category, amount, and workspace before changing it. Follow the organization’s retention and approval process for financial records.

## Reconcile totals

Use the date range and category to compare expenses with receipts and external accounting records. Exported reports are point-in-time copies and do not change when records are later edited.
