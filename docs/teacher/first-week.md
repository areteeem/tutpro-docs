# Your first week in TutPro

Use this sequence to set up a useful workspace without filling every setting first.

## 1. Set up your profile and defaults

Open **Settings** to review your name, time zone, teaching availability, lesson durations, default pricing, currency, and notification preferences. Keep per-student pricing off until you need an override. See [settings and account basics](./dashboard#settings-and-quick-actions).

## 2. Add students

Choose **Students → Add student**. Add only the details you need to schedule and contact the learner. Use groups when several students share lessons. See [manage students and groups](./students).

## 3. Plan the schedule

Go to **Schedule**, select a date or week, then choose **+** to create a lesson. Add the student or group, start time, duration, lesson topic, and any applicable price. Review time-zone and availability conflicts before saving. See [schedule lessons](./schedule).

## 4. Prepare reusable materials

Save recurring lesson structures in **Library**. Link homework templates or learning resources to a lesson when they are ready. See [lesson and homework library](./library).

## 5. Keep follow-up in one flow

After a lesson, record attendance and notes, share homework, and review submissions or grades. Log payments against the right student or lesson. See [homework and grading](./homework-grading) and [payments and reports](./payments-reports).

## 6. Invite students when ready

Issue a Student invitation from the student profile. The learner creates or signs in to their own TutPro account and accepts the invite. Read [account and invitation details](../accounts) and the [Student app guide](../student/app).
