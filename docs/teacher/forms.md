# Forms and applications

Open **Students → Forms** to work with forms and enrollment requests available in your workspace. The exact actions depend on whether you are using a personal or school account.

## Review a submitted application

Open the submission and verify the applicant and requested details. Contact the applicant if information is incomplete. Accepting an application does not necessarily create a TutPro account or complete student enrollment; follow the invitation and workspace-linking steps shown by the flow.

## Create or update a form

Use the form builder to choose the questions and intended audience. Preview the form before sharing its link. Keep questions relevant and avoid collecting information you do not need.

## Share and follow up

Share the published link privately with the intended audience. Review new submissions from the Forms list and record the next action. For an existing learner, use their student profile and enrollment invitation rather than creating a duplicate record.
