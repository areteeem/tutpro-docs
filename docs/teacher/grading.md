# Grading

Open **Grading** from the workspace navigation or **More**. Use the gradebook matrix to compare learner progress or the queue to work through items awaiting a decision.

## Find work to grade

Filter by student, item type, or grading status. Search for a specific learner or assignment. Open the selected row to read its source lesson or homework and any submission.


![Gradebook with student results and filters.](/img/screenshots/teacher-grading.png)

## Record a grade

Enter a score and verify it is within the displayed maximum. Add feedback and save. For items that cannot be graded with a numeric score, use the ungradeable control where available and explain the reason in feedback.


![Homework review with workflow and feedback status.](/img/screenshots/teacher-homework-review.png)

## Use bulk grading

Choose the bulk grading action, review the queue and selected students, then enter a consistent score/feedback decision. Confirm the apply step only after checking the selected records. You can resume a saved queue in the same browser when supported.

## Check the result

Reopen the learner’s grade or task to confirm the score and feedback were saved. A local draft or open queue is not proof that the update has synced to the remote service.
