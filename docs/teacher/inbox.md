# Team Inbox

Open **Inbox** to review tasks or requests assigned to your account. Filter by status or due date, open the item for context, and update its status when you have completed the requested action.


![Team Inbox with assigned request and task actions.](/img/screenshots/teacher-inbox.png)

A task may remain visible even if you cannot open its linked student or schedule record. Task visibility and linked-record access are separate permission checks. Ask the record owner or administrator to grant the appropriate access if you need the record to do the work.

Before closing a task, add a clear note or result if the workflow supports it. Use the inbox item’s due date and owner to coordinate handoff rather than creating a duplicate task.
