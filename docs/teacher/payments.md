# Payments

Open **Payments → Payments** to find payment records. Use filters and search to narrow by student, status, or date. The external payment provider remains the source of truth for money received; TutPro records the status and allocation you enter.

## Record or update payment status

Open the matching lesson or student record. Verify the amount, date, currency, and payment method, then choose the appropriate status. For partial or shared payments, use the Distribute workflow to allocate the amount across eligible lessons.

## Review balances

Check the student’s balance and ledger history after saving. Compare unexpected totals against the included lessons, group attendance, and any deposits or prior allocations.

## Reconcile a correction

Open the payment record and inspect its history before changing it. Follow your organization’s correction process; avoid deleting or overwriting financial context needed for reconciliation.
