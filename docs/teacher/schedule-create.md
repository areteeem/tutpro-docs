# Create a schedule entry

Use **Schedule → Add lesson** to open the schedule editor. Choose an individual student or a group, then complete the lesson details.

Before opening the editor, confirm the active personal or school workspace. In a school, choose the campus that owns the lesson; this determines its time zone and which rooms are available.

## Add an individual lesson

1. Choose the student and confirm the workspace and campus.
2. Set the date, start time, and duration. Check the time zone shown for the campus.
3. Choose the lesson type or discipline. Review the price that fills in from the selected student, duration, and pricing rules.
4. Add a topic, notes, room, and assigned teacher when those fields are available.
5. Add linked homework or learning materials if they are ready.
6. Review the summary and save. Reopen the entry to confirm the date, price, and student.

If you started from an open calendar slot, compare the date and time in the editor with the slot you clicked. If the suggested time or free-slot chips are used, select one and verify the populated date and time before continuing. Check that linked material belongs with this lesson and that the student or group is correct before saving.

## Add a group lesson

Choose **Add group lesson**, select the group, and set the time, duration, location, and lesson details. Review the participant roster before saving. Group attendance, grading, and payment records may be managed per student after the lesson.

## Create recurring lessons

If recurring lessons are enabled, choose the recurrence pattern and number of occurrences. Review each generated date for holidays, availability, room access, and campus time zone before confirming. A detected recurring pattern in the Recurring view is a suggestion; it does not create future lessons on its own.

When a generated occurrence conflicts, adjust the pattern or omit that occurrence if the controls allow it. After saving, return to the calendar and inspect the first and last dates in the series, plus any date that was adjusted manually.

## Use an open time slot

You can start from an empty calendar slot. The date and time are carried into the editor; still verify the duration, student, campus, and displayed price before saving.
