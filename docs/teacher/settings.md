# Teacher settings and navigation

Open **Settings** from the Teacher navigation. Settings are grouped by topic; the search field filters the category list. Your role and active workspace determine which categories and controls are available. The Settings page links here from both desktop and mobile.

![Teacher settings and workspace preferences.](/img/screenshots/teacher-settings.png)

## Find a setting

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select a category: **Appearance & navigation**, **General**, **Teaching & student app**, **Calendar**, **Notifications**, **Finance**, **Learning setup**, **Account & sync**, or **Data & privacy**.
3. Use **Search settings** to filter the category list by a keyword such as `currency`, `push`, or `backup`.
4. On a phone or narrow window, choose a category from the index, then use the back arrow to return to the list.

Some school roles do not have access to every personal workspace option. If a setting is missing, check the active workspace and ask a school administrator about your role.

## Change the Teacher navigation tabs

The **Appearance & navigation → Layout** section controls the configurable Teacher tabs.

1. Open **Settings → Appearance & navigation**.
2. Find the tab you want to change. **Dashboard** stays visible; other tabs can be hidden.
3. Use the grip to drag a tab, or use the up/down buttons to move it.
4. Turn **Visible** off to remove that tab from navigation. Turn it back on to restore it.
5. Edit the tab name to use a short custom label. Choose **Reset** beside a tab to restore its default name.
6. Choose **Reset layout** to restore the default order, visibility, and names.

The available list depends on your Teacher workspace. On mobile, the app keeps its main destinations and places additional pages under **More**. Hiding a page removes its navigation entry; it does not delete that page’s records.

## Choose which sections students can see

Student navigation is controlled from **Teaching & student app → Student App Integration** in Teacher Settings.

1. Open **Settings → Teaching & student app**.
2. Under **Visible tabs in student app**, check or uncheck **Schedule**, **Homework**, **Grades**, **Stats**, **Payments**, **Feed**, or **Profile**.
3. **Dashboard** is always shown and cannot be switched off.
4. **Grades** and **Stats** control the sections inside the Student **Progress** page. The Progress page appears when at least one of those sections is enabled.
5. Use **Show payment amounts** to control whether lesson prices and totals are visible to students.
6. Use **Allow lesson ratings** to allow students to rate completed lessons.
7. Allow the workspace to sync, then have the student refresh Student. Check the visible navigation and a shared payment or completed lesson to confirm the change.

These controls change what the Student app displays. They do not remove the underlying lesson, payment, grade, or feed data from the Teacher workspace.

## Configure notifications

Open **Settings → Notifications**. Student activity alerts can be controlled with the **Student updates** master switch and its individual options:

- **Homework submissions** alerts you when a student submits or updates homework.
- **Assessment submissions** alerts you when a student completes a TutPro Assess assessment. The alert appears in the Teacher notification center and is sent as a browser push notification when push is enabled for that browser.
- **Lesson ratings** alerts you when a student rates a lesson.
- **Push notifications** subscribes this browser to supported Teacher alerts. The permission row shows whether this site is allowed to display notifications in this browser. The switch reports the push subscription on this browser; each browser or device needs its own subscription.
- **Lesson reminders** and **Payment reminders** control the supported Teacher reminders.
- **Notification sound** plays a sound for supported alerts while the app is open.

To enable browser push:

1. Check the permission status row in **Settings → Notifications**.
2. If it says **Not requested**, turn on **Push notifications** and allow the browser prompt.
3. If it says **Blocked**, open the browser’s site permissions, allow notifications for `www.tutpro.org`, return to TutPro, and turn on push.
4. Confirm the row says **Allowed · Push enabled here**.
5. Keep browser and operating-system notification settings enabled. Background delivery depends on the browser, device, and network.

Student requests remain available in the request workflow, but they do not create Teacher inbox or push notifications.

## Calendar, finance, and learning setup

- **Calendar:** choose your timezone and schedule display preferences. To add a private calendar, enter a name and a private read-only iCal/ICS URL, then choose **Connect calendar**. Use the provider’s private subscription URL, not a public calendar webpage.
- **Finance:** select a currency from the currency dropdown. The monthly revenue goal is used in Statistics. Review saved lesson prices after changing the currency.
- **Learning setup:** choose a discipline, then configure levels, lesson types, grading categories, and student tag presets. These groups start collapsed; their options appear as horizontal lists. Expand a group to add, rename, reorder, or remove options.

## Account, sync, and data are separate

- **Account & sync** contains identity, sign-in, license, and cloud synchronization controls. Check the sync status and last-sync time when confirming a change on another device.
- **Data & privacy** contains backup, import/export, and privacy or deletion actions. Export a backup before a large import or replacement. Review deletion details before confirming; deleted data may not be recoverable.
