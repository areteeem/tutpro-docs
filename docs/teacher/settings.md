# Settings and workspace preferences

Open **Settings** from the account menu or workspace controls. The settings search can help locate a specific preference. Some sections depend on your role and account type.


![Teacher settings and workspace preferences.](/img/screenshots/teacher-settings.png)

## Profile and teaching defaults

Review your display name, working hours, teaching disciplines, student defaults, and preferences. Confirm the active teacher identity and school workspace before changing shared settings.

## Calendar and pricing

Set the time zone, lesson durations, recurring behavior, and default pricing. Campus defaults can affect lesson pricing in school mode. Student-specific prices or overrides appear only when enabled and authorized.

## Student app and notifications

Choose which Student sections are visible and configure available student-facing preferences and notifications. Save, then verify the corresponding section as a student test account where possible.

## Appearance and connected services

Adjust light/dark theme, language, or connected service settings. Use the sync indicator to confirm changes are persisted when the preference is shared across devices.
