# Statistics

Open **Stats** for visual summaries of lessons, learners, completion, and business activity. In a school workspace, authorized roles may see school-level comparisons and workload metrics.

## Choose a useful scope

Set the date range and any teacher or campus filters before reading the charts. Confirm whether cancelled, group, or unpaid lessons are included in a metric before comparing it with another view.


![Statistics dashboard with lesson, expense, and grade charts.](/img/screenshots/teacher-statistics.png)

## Investigate a change

Open the relevant source records—lessons, grades, payments, or expenses—to understand why a chart changed. A chart summarizes current data; it does not replace the underlying ledger.


![Chart settings and export actions.](/img/screenshots/teacher-statistics-chart-settings.png)

## Export or share carefully

Use the available export controls after checking the selected range and scope. Reports may include student or financial details, so keep downloads in approved storage.
