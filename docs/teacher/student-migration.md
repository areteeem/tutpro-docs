# Migrate students to TutPro

TutPro migration is an invitation and join process. It does not automatically copy a provider’s old records or remove access from the old provider.

## Migrate a roster in small batches

1. Review active student records and the provider workspace they belong to. Resolve duplicates and outdated contacts; do not merge records by name or email alone.
2. Pilot with a few active students. Open each student in the correct school or private-teacher workspace and issue an invitation.
3. Send the link privately and record the issue date and expiry for your own coordination. The link expires after seven days and issuing a replacement revokes the previous unused link.
4. Ask the student to sign in with their own verified TutPro email or create an account and confirm it.
5. The student reviews any profile fields they choose to copy. The new provider receives a copy only after the student accepts.
6. Confirm the student can open Student and switch to the new workspace. Reissue failed or expired invitations, then continue the next small batch.

Keep inactive roster records without creating accounts or sending invitations until access is needed.

## What transfers

The student may choose to copy profile fields shown on the invitation preview. Lessons, homework, grades, payments, and internal notes stay with their original provider. Copied price or group information is context for intake; it does not create a billing rate or automatically enroll the student in a group.

## Close access to a provider workspace

Disable only that provider’s Student connection. Do not disable the person’s TutPro account or their Teacher access. The provider’s data remains subject to its retention process. If the student leaves a workspace, they can request a new invitation to return.

## Troubleshooting

- **Wrong account:** the student chooses **Switch TutPro account** on the join page.
- **Expired or replaced link:** issue a new invitation from the correct student record and send only the current link.
- **Already connected:** verify the account and provider before taking any action. Do not rename or duplicate records as a workaround.
- **Old sid/sn link:** it no longer authenticates; issue a TutPro invitation.

> Treat an invitation URL like a one-time key. Never add it to a screenshot, public document, or support log.
