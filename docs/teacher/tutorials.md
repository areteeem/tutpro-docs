# Teacher tutorials

Use these guides to complete common teaching workflows from preparation through follow-up. Open [Teacher](https://www.tutpro.org) in the workspace where you manage your students and assignments.

## Assign homework

1. Open **Tasks** and select **Assign task**, or start from the intended student, group, or lesson.
2. Enter a clear title and instructions that tell the student what to do and how to submit it.
3. Select the intended student or group, then confirm the workspace and course.
4. Set the assigned date and due date. Check that the due date is after the assigned date.
5. Add links, files, task items, or a dedicated **Add deck** or **Add Assess** attachment when you need tracked product work.
6. Preview the student-facing version, save, and reopen the assignment to verify its recipient, dates, and attachments.
7. Check **Tasks** or **Grading** for student activity and submissions. Add a grade or feedback, save, then verify it appears in the student record.

Follow [Create and assign homework](./homework-create), [Edit homework](./homework-edit), and [Review and grade work](./grading) for details.

## Review a student's progress

1. Open the student’s profile from **Students** or select the student from the assignment or group.
2. Review the available homework, lesson, attendance, grade, and skill summaries.
3. Open a specific assignment or lesson to check the underlying status and date range.
4. Use **Stats** or **Reports** to compare progress over the period you need. Confirm the selected student, group, and date filters.
5. Discuss a next step with the student, then create or update the assignment only after reviewing the relevant context.

For Decks card-level progress, open the Decks assignment or the learner’s Decks progress view. See [Decks progress](../decks/progress).

## Use Copilot to prepare a draft

1. Open **Copilot** from Teacher, or choose an **Ask Copilot** action from a lesson, homework item, or other supported context.
2. Ask one specific question, such as “Draft a 20-minute review for this lesson” or “Suggest a follow-up for this submitted homework.”
3. Use **Attach context** to pin relevant records. Add an image only when it is needed for the request.
4. Review the response and its linked records. Check facts, student fit, tone, and any proposed dates or scores.
5. For a proposed lesson, assignment, or schedule change, open the draft in its editor, revise it, and save it yourself. Copilot suggestions do not directly create or change records.

Copilot sends your prompt and the selected or relevant workspace context to the configured AI service. An image you attach is sent for that request. Avoid including sensitive information unless your organization has approved its use. See [Copilot and lesson planning](./copilot).
