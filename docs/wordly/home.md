# Home

The home page is the starting point for a Wordly session. It shows recent documents, quick actions, and summaries of vocabulary activity. When your collection has entries, it can also show a word of the day, mastery distribution, weekly additions, review-due count, and daily goal progress.

## Start or resume work

- Choose **New Document** to begin writing. The document is created in the currently selected folder.
- Choose **New Folder** in the Folders panel to make a space for a course, language, or topic.
- Open a recent document card to continue editing it.
- Select a folder in the left panel to see the documents it contains.
- On mobile, use the bottom navigation or floating action button to reach folders, vocabulary, exercises, settings, and new documents.

## Read the progress cards

The daily goal counts vocabulary entries added today. Mastery and due-review summaries are based on vocabulary review data; the weekly chart shows words added on each day. The word of the day is selected from your current vocabulary and includes a pronunciation control and a reveal button for its translation.

Use the figures as a study prompt. Adding a word is not the same as recalling it, so open **Exercises** to practice and rate flashcards.

## Send words to Decks

Choose **Import to TutPro | Decks** to open the Decks review screen with your Wordly vocabulary. Review and edit the incoming cards in Decks, then confirm before they are saved there. See [the step-by-step handoff](./tutorials#7-send-vocabulary-to-tutpro-decks).

Wordly saves documents and folders in this browser profile. Export important work before clearing browser data or moving to a different device.
