# Home

Wordly’s home page highlights the word of the day, mastery progress, weekly activity, and recent files. Open a recent document or create a new folder/document for your study topic.

Use the mastery and weekly cards to choose what to practice next. Wordly stores its current data in the browser profile, so recent items are local to the browser and device where they were created.
