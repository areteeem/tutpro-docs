# Wordly tutorials

These walkthroughs use the controls in the current Wordly app. Your data is saved in this browser profile, so export a backup before clearing site data or moving to another browser or device.

## 1. Create a vocabulary document

1. Open [Wordly](https://wordly.tutpro.org). From the home page, choose **New Document** or open the **Folders** panel and choose **New Folder** first.
2. If you made a folder, select it and choose **New Document**. The new document opens in the editor.
3. Select the title at the top and give the document a useful name, such as “Unit 4: Travel”.
4. Type or paste reading material into the editor. Use the toolbar to add headings, lists, links, or other formatting.
5. Add a document tag in the field below the title, then press **Enter**. Tags help identify the document; vocabulary entries have their own tags.
6. Wait for the status beside the title to say **Saved** before closing or switching documents.

## 2. Add a word from your reading

1. Open a document and select a word or phrase in the editor.
2. In the popup, check the suggested translation. If it is hidden, choose **Tap to reveal translation**. Translation suggestions require an internet connection and may be unavailable or incorrect.
3. Edit the selected form if needed, for example change a plural or inflected form to its dictionary form. You can also edit the translation directly.
4. Check the sentence shown in the popup. It is saved as context for later review.
5. Choose **Add** to save the entry. Wordly highlights the selected text and adds the entry to the vocabulary list.
6. If Wordly reports a duplicate, review the existing entry instead of adding another copy. Duplicate matching follows the vocabulary scope in Settings.

To enter a term without selecting text, open the Vocabulary panel, choose the **+** control to open the add form, and fill in the word and translation. The translation suggestion is optional; review it before saving.

## 3. Add a list of words

1. Open the Vocabulary panel. On a phone, choose **Vocabulary** from the bottom navigation.
2. Choose **Import** in the Vocabulary panel and paste one entry per line.
3. Use one of these formats: `word — translation`, `word - translation`, `word,translation`, or `word<Tab>translation`. A line with only a word is accepted too.
4. Choose **Import** to add the lines. Imported entries receive the `imported` tag and are associated with the open document, if one is open.
5. Search the list and correct translations, tags, or notes that need attention.

## 4. Practice with flashcards

1. Open **Exercises** from the top bar, or press **Ctrl/⌘+Shift+X**.
2. Choose a document in the source selector, or leave the selection on all vocabulary.
3. Choose **Flashcard Review**. The deck is due-first when there are terms due for review; new terms are included when there are no due terms.
4. Read the prompt, then reveal the answer. Use **Again**, **Hard**, **Good**, or **Easy** to record how well you remembered it.
5. Continue through the deck. Your ratings update the spaced-review schedule and mastery progress.

For a quick check, choose **Type Translation**, **Type the Word**, **Multiple Choice**, **Fill in the Blanks**, **Context Clues**, **Word Bank**, **Match Pairs**, **Word Scramble**, or **Fortune Wheel**. The instructions and controls appear after you select a mode. Most modes show a score after you submit; flashcard review records a rating for each card.

## 5. Find, filter, and update vocabulary

1. Open the full Vocabulary browser with the book icon or **Ctrl/⌘+Shift+B**.
2. Search by word, translation, context, or notes.
3. Narrow the list with a tag, starred-only filter, mastery level, or source document. Clear a filter to broaden the results again.
4. Choose table, card, or compact view and sort by newest, oldest, or alphabetically.
5. Update an entry’s word or translation, star it for later, or select entries for a bulk export or delete action.
6. Use **Jump to source** on an entry to return to its document and location when that source is available.

## 6. Export a printable handout or backup

1. Choose **Export** in the top bar, or press **Ctrl/⌘+Shift+E**.
2. Select a scope: all words, a folder, a tag, one document, or selected words.
3. Choose a format: CSV, plain text, Markdown table, Anki text, Vocabulary PDF, Document PDF, or Document + Words PDF.
4. Review the options and preview, if shown. For document PDFs, select the document and choose a rich or study layout; study markers can add lines or word markers.
5. Choose **Export**. For print formats, use the browser print dialog to save as PDF or print.
6. Open the downloaded file and check it before sharing. Store a backup somewhere separate from the browser profile.

## 7. Send vocabulary to TutPro Decks

1. Return to the Wordly home page.
2. Choose **Import to TutPro | Decks**. The link includes the current vocabulary list and its translations, context sentences, and notes.
3. In Decks, review the incoming cards. Rename the deck, edit or remove rows, and check the content before confirming.
4. Choose **Confirm and import** only when the review is complete. The cards are saved to Decks after confirmation.

The handoff opens Decks in a separate product and may require a teacher account. Very large vocabularies may exceed browser URL limits; export a file or split the collection if the handoff does not open correctly.
