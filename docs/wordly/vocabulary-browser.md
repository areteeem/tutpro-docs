# Vocabulary browser

Open the full Vocabulary browser with the book icon or **Ctrl/⌘+Shift+B**. Use the panel on the right for quick capture and recent vocabulary, or open the full-screen browser to work with the whole collection.

## Search and narrow the list

Search matches words, translations, context sentences, and notes. Filter by tag, starred entries, mastery, or source document, then sort by newest, oldest, or alphabetically. Switch between table, cards, and compact layouts to suit the task. Clear filters to return to the wider collection.

## Edit and review entries

Edit the word or translation, add or remove tags, star a term, or delete an entry. In the full browser, select multiple entries for bulk actions. Use **Jump to source** to reopen the document at the captured text when a source location is available.

A vocabulary word can be associated with more than one occurrence in documents. When you collect a duplicate, Wordly can keep the word as one entry and record another occurrence rather than creating an unrelated copy.

## Add words without the editor

Use the **+** control in the side panel to open the add form for a word and optional translation. The panel may suggest a translation; check it before saving. Use **Import** to paste several lines in `word — translation`, `word - translation`, `word,translation`, or tab-separated form. Imported terms receive an `imported` tag.

## Copy or export

Copy the visible list or table from the side panel, or export CSV. In the full browser, CSV export follows the current search and filters. For other formats and document PDFs, open the dedicated [Export dialog](./practice-and-export).
