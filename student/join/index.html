Student accounts

Join a school or teacher

Ask your teacher or school for a personal invitation. All account creation and sign-in happens at www.tutpro.org.

  1. Open the invitation from your teacher. A new invitation is valid for seven days and replaces any earlier unused invitation for the same student record.
  2. Sign in to your existing TutPro account, or create one and confirm your email. If you also teach, use the same account.
  3. Review the provider and any profile fields you choose to copy from another workspace. You may choose not to copy an existing profile.
  4. Confirm Join. TutPro opens your Student app with this provider available in the workspace switcher.

Wrong account? Select “Switch TutPro account” on the join page. Your invitation stays ready while you sign in again.

If the link does not work

  • Expired or replaced: Ask your teacher for a new invitation. Only the latest unused link works.
  • Already used: Sign in to the account that accepted it. If the wrong person accepted it, contact the provider; do not share account passwords.
  • Different email: Switch to the invited email account or ask the teacher to reissue the link to your verified address.
  • Old sid/sn link: These links no longer grant access. Ask for a new TutPro invitation.
  • Temporary problem: Retry after a moment. If it persists, send your provider the error text, not the secret invitation URL.

Privacy

The new provider gets only the Student Profile information you approve on the preview. Lessons, homework, grades, payments and internal notes are not transferred. Copied price or group information is context for intake, not a new billing rate or automatic group enrollment.
