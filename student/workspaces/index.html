Student workspaces

Study with more than one provider

Each school or private teacher is a separate workspace. Use the switcher in Student to view one provider’s schedule, homework, feedback and profile at a time.

Join another provider

Ask the receiving provider for a new invitation. Accepting it adds a workspace; it does not remove your previous one. Profile information is copied only after you review and approve it. Later changes in one workspace do not silently change another.

Leave a provider

In the Student Profile, choose the provider and select Remove school or teacher. Your access to that workspace ends immediately. The provider keeps its own records under its retention rules and is notified of your departure. To return, request a new invitation.

If a provider disables access

Only that provider’s workspace becomes inactive. It stops loading in Student and locally cached portal data is cleared. Your global TutPro account, other Student workspaces and Teacher suite remain available. Contact the provider to ask for renewed access.

A school does not own or disable your TutPro account. Never share your password or a one-time invitation link.
